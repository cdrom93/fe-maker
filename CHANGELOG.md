# Changelog

## 0.1.0-prealpha (since 07/11/2026; the alpha is not released yet)

First public test version, Windows first. See [the guide](docs/guide.en.md) for what you need and the known limits.

- **Workflow:** the project's actions (back, undo, test, project menu) in the top bar; the Campaign graph first, then one chapter's units, map and dialogues in the Chapters tab; explanations in help bubbles next to what they explain.
- **Characters:** starting level, average stats at a chosen level with promotions, affinities with the game's icons, portrait picked by its picture, gender following the class. A named ally's level is set on their sheet.
- **Battle colours:** a character's own colours by class, drawn from their portrait or painted on the sprite, with zoom, swapped by dragging, and your own colours kept when you try others.
- **Classes:** stats, animations with their empty movements marked, map sprites, generic portrait among the game's class cards and portraits.
- **Items:** magic weapons of any type (with a warning when a class has no ranged animation, and a "Strikes at range" filter in the animation library), icon picker, weapon effect, stat bonuses, rank points, properties (brave, unbreakable, no counter...), spell taken from another weapon, Use text; any item (staff, monster weapon, potion, key...) copied into a new one that acts like its model or like another item, with its own amount (HP healed, bonuses or maluses on several stats that fade each turn); a new type brings what the engine expects of it.
- **Adding to the game:** characters, weapons, classes and chapters on the free slots of the game's tables; blank project starting from one empty chapter; choice of what a project keeps of the game.
- **Maps:** painting, terrain brushes that join by themselves, filling empty space, buildings as blocks, tile sheets and palette colours (picked on the map and recoloured by shades, as for characters), resizing on all four sides, map changes on any map, villages, chests, doors and shops set in a list of map objects, Tiled import and export.
- **Chapters:** units (dropped item, computed stats, weapon ranks), units listed turn by turn with a turn shown on the map, event groups for cutscenes, automatic levels, enemy levels by difficulty, objectives with the game's icons, reach, staff range and danger zone; settings folded at the bottom of the sheet: convoy, preparations, fog, weather, battle background picked from a gallery, starting camera.
- **Events:** the acting unit's portrait (the one visiting a village) in dialogues; visual sequences, condition blocks, Yes / No questions and memories of the player's choices; events on a turn or a range of turns, on Talk, on an area or on a memory; commands for stats, HP, AI, side, leaving and rejoining the party, tile changes, weather, fog, screen tint, counters, sounds and warps; dialogues; quotes (battle, death) kept apart from events; supports.
- **Feedback:** a more compact Ticket panel; an optional name joined to the tickets, or a GitHub sign-in checked by the ticket relay; missions for the testers in the Ticket panel, each with three questions, the answers sent as a ticket; tickets can join the last actions and errors of the session (shown before sending); the Ticket button sends to the developers without an account (screenshots, the project on request), keeps unsent tickets to send again and shows the replies.
- **Music:** the phase song comes back after a battle whatever song you chose; imported songs in the sound room; a character's theme in either camp; a Music tab with the game's songs and MIDI files imported from your PC or songs from FE-Repo-Music (heard from their recording before choosing), heard in FE Maker with the game's instruments or in General MIDI order (NIMAP2); each chapter's phase, opening and victory music, battle music by situation, boss themes, the sequences' Music command, the fixed screens (title, preparations, shops, game over, epilogue...); game options for 16 tracks and 12 notes at once and a full drum kit.
- **Resources:** portraits, battle animations and map sprites from FE-Repo or your PC, with their authors in the project's credits.
- **Skills:** over two hundred skills ported from C-SkillSys: growths and experience (Blossom, Aptitude, Paragon, Discipline), passive (stat bonuses, Defiant, Push, Fortress, Blows, Stances, weapon-type bonuses, bonuses by HP, by the foe, by range, terrain or turn, Heavy and Flashing Blade, Wrath, Vantage, Desperation; the order of the rounds as C-SkillSys's table, both units able to double, the forecast's x2 on each side: Quick Riposte, Bold, Reckless and Wary Fighter, Gale Wings, Dazzle, Moonlight, Last Word, Double Lion...; surviving a deadly hit (Inori, Sturdy), statuses inflicted (Petrify, Poison Point, Enrage, Effect Spore, Black Magic), Resolve, Lucky Seven, Triangle Adept, Skybreaker, the game's Slayer for anyone, Armsthrift, Corrosion, Pickup, Bargain and Deal (shop prices); after the attack, Savage Blow, Thunderstorm and Lunge; auras of the units nearby (Spur, Drive, Charm, Bond, Hex, Anathema, Tantivy, Inner Flame...) and heals at the start of the turn (Imbue, Relief, Camaraderie, Amaterasu...), the enemy AI's targets (Shade, Shade+, Provoke); hit, crit and damage: Hawkeye, Killing Machine, Deadeye, Counter and Counter Magic (damage sent back), Nihil (the foe's skills cancelled in battle), Devil's Pact, Luck and Whim, Axefaith, Nullify, Rightful King, Rightful God and Hero (activation rates), Fortune, Infinity Edge, Bane, Mercy, Dragon Skin, Multiscale, No Guard, Counter Roar...; a "?" icon for skills without one, the classic Skill System's icon for eight of them) and triggered by Skill % (Luna, Corona, Sol, Aether, Ignis, Colossus, Pavise, Aegis), extra hits (Adept, Astra), the triggered skill's name and icon shown in battle animations (its icon in map battles), up to 3 on each character and class sheet (chosen in a window showing each skill's icon, name and effect, by category, with a search), up to 4 more learned by level (character and class sheets) or given and taken by events, saved with the game, a popup when one is learned; the game's Canto, Steal, Lock Touch, Crit +15 and Lethality can be given to anyone, map skills Pass (through the foes), Canto+, Renewal and Galeforce (every time, once a turn, with the game's status heal flash), the unit-menu commands Pivot, Reposition, Shove, Smite, Swap and the Rallies (+4 to the allies around until the next player turn, Movement too), Celerity (Movement +2) (the units slide on the map), shown with their icons on the unit's status screen with R help; the game's class abilities appear there as skills.
- **Game options:** by category: HP bars, talk bubbles and warnings on the enemies holding an effective or a critical weapon, a small bag on the enemies that drop an item; Fixes (on in every new project): seven bugs of the original game (enemy control after a suspend on a trap, Pierce freeze, battle forecast graphics, camera past the map, Fimbulvetr freeze, trap limit, Light Arrow trap), enemy healers ignoring units at full HP, promoted enemies not too weak in easy and normal (working with every weather), damage and healing numbers in battle animations, healing preview when choosing a staff's target, L held speeds up battle animations, a fallen unit's items go to the convoy, beginner mode, quick experience and level up, acting after Talk, growth rates on the status screen (Select), monster weapons (claws, fangs) showing their battle stats like weapons, no story intro, a Staff & Dance line in the Configuration menu; Strength and Magic as two stats (on in every new project: tomes, magic swords at range and staves use Magic; a Mag line on the status and level-up screens and in FE Maker's sheets).
- **Updates:** a packaged FE Maker offers a newer published version in its top bar and installs it on one click (projects and settings kept), then starts again.
- **Testing and sharing:** "Test this chapter" on the route's own chapters and on scene-only chapters; chapter 7's army entering at the right places once the game's code changed; before a release, a script plays every in-game test and a reference project chapter by chapter, with a one-line report per test; a failed build says why in plain words (text, file and line, missing file, game too big...) with a link to the tab concerned; test in mGBA, built on Windows without Docker (FE Maker downloads its own build tools, about 25 MB, and makes the first build in the background), each build's duration shown with the previous one, export as a `.ups` patch, each build's duration shown with the previous one and a history to compare, builds with no change about twice as fast without Docker; each project's `CREDITS.txt` says that FE Maker is largely developed with generative AI.
- **Projects** carry over between versions: converted when opened, after a copy in `backups/`; a project from a newer version is refused.
