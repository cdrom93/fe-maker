# Changelog

## 0.1.0-prealpha (since 07/11/2026; the alpha is not released yet)

First public test version, Windows first. See [the guide](docs/guide.en.md) for what you need and the known limits.

- **Workflow:** the project's actions (back, undo, test, project menu) in the top bar; the Campaign graph first, then one chapter's units, map and dialogues in the Chapters tab; explanations in help bubbles next to what they explain.
- **Characters:** starting level, average stats at a chosen level with promotions, affinities with the game's icons, portrait picked by its picture, gender following the class. A named ally's level is set on their sheet.
- **Battle colours:** a character's own colours by class, drawn from their portrait or painted on the sprite, with zoom, swapped by dragging, and your own colours kept when you try others.
- **Classes:** stats, animations with their empty movements marked, map sprites, generic portrait among the game's class cards and portraits.
- **Items:** magic weapons of any type (with a warning when a class has no ranged animation, and a "Strikes at range" filter in the animation library), icon picker, weapon effect, stat bonuses, rank points, properties (brave, unbreakable, no counter...), spell taken from another weapon, Use text; any item (staff, monster weapon, potion, key...) copied into a new one that acts like its model or like another item, with its own amount (HP healed, bonuses or maluses on several stats that fade each turn); a new type brings what the engine expects of it.
- **Adding to the game:** characters, weapons, classes and chapters on the free slots of the game's tables; blank project starting from one empty chapter; choice of what a project keeps of the game.
- **Maps:** painting, terrain brushes that join by themselves, filling empty space, buildings as blocks, tile sheets and palette colours (picked on the map and recoloured by shades, as for characters), resizing on all four sides, map changes on any map, villages, chests, doors and shops set in a list of map objects, Tiled import and export.
- **Chapters:** units (dropped item, computed stats, weapon ranks), units listed turn by turn with a turn shown on the map, event groups for cutscenes, automatic levels, enemy levels by difficulty, objectives with the game's icons, reach, staff range and danger zone; settings folded at the bottom of the sheet: convoy, preparations, fog, weather, battle background picked from a gallery, starting camera.
- **Events:** visual sequences, condition blocks, Yes / No questions and memories of the player's choices; events on a turn or a range of turns, on Talk, on an area or on a memory; commands for stats, HP, AI, side, leaving and rejoining the party, tile changes, weather, fog, screen tint, counters, sounds and warps; dialogues; quotes (battle, death) kept apart from events; supports.
- **Feedback:** the Ticket button sends to the developers without an account (screenshots, the project on request), keeps unsent tickets to send again and shows the replies.
- **Resources:** portraits, battle animations and map sprites from FE-Repo or your PC, with their authors in the project's credits.
- **Skills:** about thirty skills ported from C-SkillSys: passive (stat bonuses, Defiant, Blows, Wrath, Vantage, Desperation) and triggered by Skill % (Luna, Corona, Sol, Aether, Ignis, Colossus, Pavise, Aegis), extra hits (Adept, Astra), the triggered skill's name and icon shown in battle animations (its icon in map battles), up to 3 on each character and class sheet, up to 4 more learned by level (character and class sheets) or given and taken by events, saved with the game, a popup when one is learned; the game's Canto, Steal, Lock Touch, Crit +15 and Lethality can be given to anyone, map skills Canto+, Renewal and Galeforce, the unit-menu commands Pivot, Reposition, Shove and Swap, shown with their icons on the unit's status screen with R help; the game's class abilities appear there as skills.
- **Game options:** by category: HP bars and talk bubbles (working with every weather), damage and healing numbers in battle animations, healing preview when choosing a staff's target, L held speeds up battle animations, a fallen unit's items go to the convoy, beginner mode, quick experience and level up, acting after Talk, growth rates on the status screen (Select), no story intro, a Staff & Dance line in the Configuration menu; Strength and Magic as two stats (on in every new project: tomes, magic swords at range and staves use Magic; a Mag line on the status and level-up screens and in FE Maker's sheets).
- **Testing and sharing:** test in mGBA, built on Windows without Docker (FE Maker downloads its own build tools, about 25 MB, and makes the first build in the background), export as a `.ups` patch; each project's `CREDITS.txt` says that FE Maker is largely developed with generative AI.
- **Projects** carry over between versions: converted when opened, after a copy in `backups/`; a project from a newer version is refused.
